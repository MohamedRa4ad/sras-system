ENGINEERING MEETS INNOVATION

ENGINEERED TO UNDERSTAND MOVEMENT.

The project combines biomedical engineering, embedded systems, and digital software to support physiotherapy professionals. It is a research and development effort — not a certified medical device, and not designed to diagnose conditions on its own.

  1. BIOMEDICAL SENSORS

    Capture signals related to muscle activity and movement.

  2. SMART HARDWARE

    Embedded electronics that acquire and transmit the data.

  3. DATA PROCESSING

    Software that filters and organizes raw signals.

  4. DIGITAL VISUALIZATION

    Clear interfaces that help professionals evaluate results.
